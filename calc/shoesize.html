How it works

Shoe sizing systems don't share a common base unit, so this tool converts your size to foot length in centimeters first, then maps that length to the equivalent US, UK and EU sizes using standard sizing charts.

Frequently asked questions

Why do results vary slightly between brands?
Sizing charts are standardized guidelines — actual fit varies by brand, last shape and country of manufacture, so always check a specific brand's chart when possible.
