How it works

Multiply length by width to get area. This tool converts the result to square feet, square meters and square yards, and — if you enter a material cost — estimates the total price for flooring, tile or paint coverage.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure an irregular room?
Split the room into rectangles, calculate each area separately, then add them together.
