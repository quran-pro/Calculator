How it works

This tool uses the Rothfusz regression, the formula behind the U.S. National Weather Service heat index tables, which combines temperature and relative humidity into a single "feels-like" value that reflects how the body perceives heat.

Frequently asked questions

Why does humidity matter for temperature?
High humidity slows sweat evaporation, the body's main cooling mechanism, so the air feels hotter than the thermometer reading.
