How it works

Volume in cubic yards = length × width × depth (converted to feet) ÷ 27. Weight is estimated using an average density for the gravel type, since different materials compact to different weights per cubic yard.

Frequently asked questions

How deep should a gravel driveway be?
A base layer of 4–6 inches is typical, sometimes with a compacted sub-base beneath it for heavier traffic.
