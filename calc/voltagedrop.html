How it works

Voltage drop = 2 × K × I × L ÷ 1000, using the resistance-per-1000-ft constant (K) for copper at each AWG size, current (I) in amps and one-way distance (L) in feet. The result is doubled because current travels out and back. Most electrical codes recommend keeping drop under 3% for branch circuits.

Frequently asked questions

What's a safe voltage drop percentage?
3% or less is the common recommendation for branch circuits, and 5% total for the combined feeder and branch circuit.
