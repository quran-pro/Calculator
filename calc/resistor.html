How it works

The first two color bands give a two-digit number, the third band is a multiplier applied to that number, and the fourth band states the tolerance — the manufacturer's guaranteed accuracy of the printed value.

Frequently asked questions

What about 5-band resistors?
5-band resistors use three digit bands instead of two for extra precision, followed by a multiplier and tolerance band, using the same color-to-number scheme.
