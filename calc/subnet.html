How it works

The CIDR prefix defines how many leading bits of the 32-bit address are the network portion. This tool applies the resulting subnet mask to your IP address to find the network address, broadcast address, the range of usable host addresses, and the total number of hosts the subnet can hold.

Frequently asked questions

Why subtract 2 from the host count?
The first address in a subnet is the network address and the last is the broadcast address — neither can be assigned to a device.
