How this age calculator works

This age calculator finds your exact age from your date of birth by counting complete years, then complete months within the current year, then remaining days — the same method used to state age on official documents. It also totals the exact number of days and weeks you've been alive, and counts down to your next birthday.

Why calculate your exact age?

Beyond simple curiosity, an exact age calculation is useful for eligibility checks (school enrollment, retirement, voting), legal and medical forms, or working out someone's age at a specific historical or future date rather than today.

Frequently asked questions

How is age calculated from date of birth?
Age is found by counting complete years, then complete months within the current year, then remaining days between the birth date and today (or any chosen date).
Does it account for leap years?
Yes. Because the calculation is based on real calendar dates, leap years are handled automatically.
Can I calculate age on a future or past date?
Yes — set "Age as of" to any date to see age at that point in time, past or future.
How many days old am I?
Enter your date of birth and the result panel shows your total days lived alongside years, months and days.
