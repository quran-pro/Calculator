How it works

Density equals mass divided by volume (ρ = m/V). This tool rearranges that formula so you can solve for whichever of the three values you're missing.

Frequently asked questions

What units should I use?
Any consistent set works — the default here is kilograms and liters, giving density in kg/L (equivalent to g/mL or g/cm³).
