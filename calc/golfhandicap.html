How it works

Each round's score differential = (score − course rating) × 113 ÷ slope rating. The handicap index is the average of your differentials, multiplied by 0.96 — the same core approach used in the World Handicap System, simplified here for a small number of rounds.

Frequently asked questions

How many rounds does an official handicap need?
The official World Handicap System typically uses your best 8 of your last 20 rounds — this tool is a simplified estimate for a handful of recent rounds.
