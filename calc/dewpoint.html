How it works

This calculator uses the Magnus approximation, a widely used formula that estimates dew point from air temperature and relative humidity. A higher dew point means more moisture in the air and a muggier feel.

Frequently asked questions

What dew point feels comfortable?
Below 60°F (16°C) generally feels comfortable; above 65°F (18°C) starts to feel muggy, and above 75°F (24°C) feels oppressive.
