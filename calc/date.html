How this date calculator works

Choose a starting date, then add or subtract a number of days, weeks, months or years. Month and year math preserves the day of month where possible, rolling to the end of shorter months automatically — so adding one month to January 31 correctly lands on the last day of February.

Common uses

People use this tool to find due dates, contract or warranty expiry dates, project deadlines, and "N days from today" or "N days before a date" questions without counting a calendar by hand.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add days to a date?
Pick a start date, choose "Add", select a unit and amount, then calculate — the resulting date and weekday appear instantly.
What happens with month or year math near the end of a month?
The day of month is preserved where possible; if the target month is shorter, the date rolls to that month's last valid day.
Can I subtract time instead?
Yes — switch the operation to "Subtract" to go backward from the start date.
