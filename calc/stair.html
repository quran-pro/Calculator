How it works

The number of steps is the total rise divided by your target riser height, rounded to the nearest whole number — the actual riser height is then recalculated so every step is identical. Most residential codes call for risers between 7 and 7.75 inches, and the rule of thumb "2 × riser + tread = 24–25 inches" gives a comfortable tread depth.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the calculator adjust my riser height?
Steps must be equal height for safety, so the exact rise is divided evenly across a whole number of steps.
What tread depth should I use?
If you don't enter one, the calculator suggests a depth using the 2×riser + tread ≈ 25 in comfort rule.
