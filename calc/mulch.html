How it works

Volume = area × depth. Since mulch depth is measured in inches, the tool converts depth to feet, multiplies by the bed area to get cubic feet, then divides by 27 to get cubic yards (bulk mulch is usually sold by the yard, bagged mulch by 2-cubic-foot bags).

Frequently asked questions

How deep should mulch be?
2–4 inches is standard for weed suppression and moisture retention without smothering roots.
